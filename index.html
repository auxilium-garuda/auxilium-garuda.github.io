Not a mockup. It already works.

Our ground control runs today with simulated ArduPilot drones: area planning, a live fleet, confirm and drop, and an automatic return to base. This is a real screenshot from a simulated mission over Krefeld.

  1. 1Each drone reports battery, satellites and flight time. D-02 has finished sector B and landed.
  2. 2Every confirmed find gets a marker on the map, numbered F1, F2, F3 in the order they were confirmed.
  3. 3Finds are listed with coordinates and the time of the drop, ready to copy or open in OpenStreetMap.
