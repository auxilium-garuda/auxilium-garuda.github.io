03Ground control

Not a mockup. It already works.

This is our real ground control software in a simulated mission over Krefeld: four simulated ArduPilot drones, each searching its own sector. The interface is in German, built for German emergency teams.

  1. 1The whole fleet at a glance: battery, satellites, flight time and what each drone is doing right now.
  2. 2Each drone flies its own sector. Confirmed finds are marked F1, F2 on the map, and supplies are dropped there.
  3. 3When the camera spots a person, the drone waits. The photo and AI confidence appear here, and a responder confirms or rejects.

Only the right people can fly

Every user signs in with a role: observer, operator or incident commander. Buttons a role is not allowed to use stay hidden, and the ground station rejects those commands anyway.

Every command is logged with who sent it, including failed logins and rejected commands.
